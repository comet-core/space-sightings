### Observation Log
- **Atmospheric Seeing:** Antoniadi II (Clear mountain air above thermal boundary)
- **Target Altitude:** +52°18' near southern meridian transit
- **Spectral Class:** A7V (Luminous white main-sequence dwarf)
- **Stellar Kinematics:** Known oblate spheroid spinning at ~286 km/s (~9 hr equatorial period)

Altair forms the southern vertex of the famed Summer Triangle along with Vega (Lyra) and Deneb (Cygnus). The photon baseline recorded on the mobile CMOS sensor represents light emitted 16.73 years ago, traversing approximately 1.58 × 10¹⁴ kilometers of interstellar space.
